Managing Your Plan

The Plan page in Settings lets you view your current plan, upgrade to a paid plan, and activate a License Key.

Subscription plans

Plan Usage limit Price
Free 21 inputs/day Free
NKTg AI Plus 55 inputs/day $2 USD/month (~50,000 VND)
NKTg AI Pro Unlimited inputs $5 USD/month (~125,000 VND)

All plans use the same NKTg AI system with full features and the same algorithm. The only difference is the number of inputs allowed per day.

Viewing plan status

If you're on a paid plan, the Plan page will display:

  • Current plan
  • Subscription status
  • Expiry or renewal date
  • Daily usage limit for your plan

Upgrading your plan

  1. Log in to your NKTg AI account
  2. Open Settings
  3. Select Plan
  4. Choose the plan you want
  5. Choose a payment method:
    • Pay via VietQR (for users in Vietnam)
    • Pay via Lemon Squeezy (international payments)
  6. Complete the payment as instructed

Once payment is confirmed, the plan is automatically activated on your account.

Activating a License Key

  1. Open Settings
  2. Select Plan
  3. Enter your License Key in the corresponding field
  4. Click Activate

If the code is valid, your plan is activated or renewed immediately on your account.

Which plan should I choose?

  • Free: Suitable for trying out the app and basic needs
  • NKTg AI Plus: Suitable for users who process documents regularly and need a higher daily limit
  • NKTg AI Pro: Suitable for users with continuous, unlimited usage needs

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or continue renewing your plan at any time via Settings → Plan.

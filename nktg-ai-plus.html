What is NKTg AI Plus?

Information about the NKTg AI Plus paid subscription.

NKTg AI Plus is a paid subscription for users who need to use NKTg AI more frequently. Compared to the Free plan, NKTg AI Plus raises the daily usage limit from 21 to 55 inputs per day. Aside from the usage limit, all features, algorithms, and analysis results are identical across plans.

Benefits of NKTg AI Plus

  • Up to 55 analyses per day
  • Full access to all of NKTg AI's features
  • Analysis powered by the same NKTg Law algorithm
  • AMP, DAMP, and STABLE classification
  • Core Content extraction
  • Full access to Extraction and Addition modes

Note: NKTg AI Plus does not unlock any new features. The only difference is the daily usage limit.

Subscription details

  • Price: $2 USD/month
  • Usage limit: 55 analyses per day
  • Platform: NKTg AI runs directly in the browser at https://nktg.org/

How do I subscribe?

  1. Open your browser and go to https://nktg.org/
  2. Log in or create a new account
  3. Open Settings → select Plan
  4. Under NKTg AI Plus, choose a payment method:
    • Pay via VietQR (for users in Vietnam)
    • Pay via Lemon Squeezy (international payments)
  5. Choose your subscription duration
  6. Complete the payment

Once the system confirms payment, your NKTg AI Plus license is activated automatically.

Usage limit

NKTg AI Plus allows up to 55 analyses per day. The limit resets daily. Once you reach the limit, you can continue the next day or upgrade to NKTg AI Pro for unlimited use.

Payment

  • VietQR: For users in Vietnam. After scanning the QR code and transferring funds, the system automatically confirms and activates your license.
  • Lemon Squeezy: For international payments. Once payment succeeds, the system automatically activates your license.

Managing your subscription

Log in at https://nktg.org/, open Settings → Plan to view:

  • Your current plan
  • Your license duration
  • Your daily usage limit
  • How many uses you've used today

Payment issues

If you've paid but your account still shows the Free plan:

  1. Make sure you're logged in with the account used for payment
  2. Wait a few minutes for the system to confirm
  3. Refresh the page or log in again
  4. If it's still not activated, contact support with your transaction details

Renewal and cancellation

To renew, repeat the payment steps. NKTg AI Plus does not auto-renew; once it expires, the account reverts to the Free plan.

Privacy

  • Text is processed directly in the browser via WebAssembly
  • Text never leaves the device
  • No server is used to process content
  • Text is not stored in the cloud
  • Usage history is stored locally on the device

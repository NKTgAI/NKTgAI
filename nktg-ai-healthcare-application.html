How do doctors and healthcare staff use NKTg AI?

Quickly identify diagnoses, treatment directives, contraindications, and conclusions in medical records and professional guidelines

In a healthcare setting, every decision needs to be based on accurate information. Medical records may include medical history, test results, imaging diagnoses, treatment progress, physician directives, and other related documents.

NKTg AI helps users recognize a document's structure before reading, so they can choose the right approach for each type of document.

Scenario 1: Reading a medical record

After analysis, NKTg AI shows AMP = 72%, indicating the record focuses on treatment decisions and clinical actions.

Read the Core Content first, which usually contains:

  • Confirmed diagnosis
  • Treatment directive
  • Medication or treatment method
  • Dosage or treatment plan
  • Follow-up requirements

Scenario 2: Reading a treatment guideline

Analysis result: DAMP = 69%, showing the document focuses on:

  • Applicable conditions
  • Contraindications
  • Warnings
  • Adverse effects
  • Clinical explanation
  • Clinical evidence

In this case, focus on reading the contraindications, indication conditions, warnings, and monitoring sections during treatment.

Scenario 3: Reading a professional procedure

Analysis result: AMP = 50%, DAMP = 48% → STABLE state.

The document is built to standardize a professional procedure, with no dominant Core Content. Users should read step by step to ensure safety and professional quality.

How to use NKTg AI in daily work

  • Step 1: Observe the AMP and DAMP scores.
  • Step 2: Choose the right reading approach:
    • AMP > 55% → Read the Core Content first to quickly grasp the diagnosis, treatment directive, conclusion, and management plan.
    • DAMP > 55% → Focus on applicable conditions, contraindications, warnings, and clinical evidence.
    • AMP ≈ DAMP (STABLE) → Read step by step or section by section.
  • Step 3: Continue reviewing related sections.

Value of NKTg AI for healthcare staff

NKTg AI does not make diagnoses, does not replace a doctor, and does not recommend treatment methods. Its value is helping users quickly access the necessary information in the original document:

  • In medical records: quickly identify the diagnosis, treatment directive, and conclusion.
  • In professional guidelines: recognize applicable conditions, contraindications, and warnings.
  • In professional procedures: recognize the STABLE state and read step by step.

Conclusion

In Healthcare, what matters isn't just accessing information quickly, but accessing the right content that supports a clinical decision. NKTg AI helps doctors and healthcare staff understand a document's structure from the start using the AMP, DAMP, and STABLE metrics, helping them choose the right way to read each type of document.

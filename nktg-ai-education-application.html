How do students use NKTg AI?

Quickly identify the main idea of study material while keeping the original sentence intact

Students often have to read a large volume of material: textbooks, lecture notes, reference material, research papers, and academic regulations. The biggest challenge isn't a lack of material — it's not knowing where to start.

NKTg AI helps learners answer these questions by decoding the language structure of the material, rather than rewriting or paraphrasing the content.

Scenario 1: Studying a chapter of a textbook

Analysis result: AMP = 69%, indicating the chapter focuses on a specific concept or conclusion.

Read the Core Content first — usually the sentence that most clearly expresses the core idea the author wants the reader to remember.

Importantly, NKTg AI keeps the original sentence from the textbook unchanged, helping learners access the author's own wording.

Scenario 2: Reviewing before an exam

Analysis result: DAMP = 64%, indicating the material mainly consists of:

  • Concept explanations
  • Analysis
  • Illustrative examples
  • Conditions
  • Exceptions
  • Comparisons between cases

In this case, learners should read all the conditions and examples rather than focusing only on a single Core Content sentence.

Scenario 3: Reading a textbook or guidance material

Analysis result: AMP = 50%, DAMP = 48% → STABLE state.

The material is built step by step, with no dominant Core Content. Learners should study section by section or step by step to fully grasp the knowledge and skills.

How to use NKTg AI while studying

  • Step 1: Observe the AMP and DAMP scores.
  • Step 2: Choose the right way to study:
    • AMP > 55% → Read the Core Content first to quickly grasp the main idea.
    • DAMP > 55% → Focus on the explanations, examples, conditions, and exceptions.
    • AMP ≈ DAMP (STABLE) → Study section by section or step by step.
  • Step 3: Continue reading the full material for deeper understanding and retention.

Value of NKTg AI for learners

NKTg AI does not replace teachers and does not generate new study content. Its value is helping students access the most important information in the original material:

  • In lessons with a clear focal point: quickly identify the Core Content.
  • In analytical material: know to spend time on conditions, examples, and exceptions.
  • In step-by-step textbooks: recognize the STABLE state and study step by step.

Importantly, the Core Content is always kept verbatim from the original material, helping learners engage with exactly what the author conveyed.

Conclusion

Effective studying isn't just reading more — it's correctly identifying the material's main focus. NKTg AI helps students understand a lesson's structure from the start using the AMP, DAMP, and STABLE metrics, helping them choose the right approach.

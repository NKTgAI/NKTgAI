How to use the Addition feature

Addition is the feature that expands and completes text based on the user's original content. Unlike Extraction (distillation), Addition doesn't remove information — it adds relevant content, making the text fuller, clearer, and more valuable, while keeping the original topic intact.

During expansion, NKTg AI uses its Semantic Energy algorithm to analyze the text structure. The system prioritizes developing content with high AMP to increase actionability, implementation detail, and practical value.

How to use it

Step 1. Select Addition mode

Click the Addition button on the main interface.

Step 2. Enter or upload text

You can paste text directly or upload a file (TXT, DOCX, PDF, PNG, JPG, WEBP). Then click Submit.

Step 3. Choose the expansion level

Addition offers 3 modes:

  • Refined: Light expansion, adding the necessary content to make the text more coherent. Suitable for emails, websites, and short content.
  • Expanded: Moderate expansion, developing related ideas further, suitable for SEO, reports, and marketing.
  • Comprehensive: The strongest expansion, producing a full, in-depth text, suitable for technical documents, research, and training material.

Semantic Energy analysis

After processing, NKTg AI displays a chart of the text structure:

  • AMP (%) – action, outcome, and implementation content
  • DAMP (%) – condition, explanation, and evaluation content
  • STABLE – balanced, neutral content

If AMP > 55%, the text has high semantic energy and the Core Sentence carries high reliability.

Result analysis

The Analysis section provides:

  • Token count before and after expansion
  • The Expansion ratio
  • Character count before and after processing

Underlined words

Some words/phrases are underlined to indicate their contribution to DAMP. Hovering over them shows a suggestion for you to consider adjusting. You can keep them as-is, replace them with stronger words, or rewrite the sentence to increase AMP. NKTg AI does not automatically edit the content.

Copying the result

Once Addition is finished, you can click Copy to copy the entire result, or quickly switch between Refined, Expanded, and Comprehensive modes to compare them.

When should you use Addition?

  • Expanding an article while staying on-topic
  • Enriching website/blog content
  • Writing more detailed product/service descriptions
  • Drafting guides, reports, or training materials
  • Building in-depth content from a short draft
  • Increasing informational value without changing the core meaning

Addition develops content from the original text, making it fuller, more coherent, and richer in information, while maintaining the Semantic Energy structure according to NKTg AI's algorithm.

How do project managers use NKTg AI?

Quickly identify tasks, owners, targets, and timelines in meeting minutes or plans

A project can generate hundreds of documents over its lifecycle: meeting minutes, implementation plans, progress reports, task lists, change requests, and summary reports. NKTg AI helps project managers quickly identify this important information before reading the full document.

Scenario 1: Reading project meeting minutes

Analysis result: AMP = 78%, showing the meeting reached many concrete decisions and actions.

Read the Core Content first, which usually identifies simultaneously:

  • The work that must be done
  • The responsible person
  • The target to reach
  • The completion timeline
"The development team will complete the Beta version by August 15; the technical lead is responsible for acceptance testing and weekly progress reporting."

Scenario 2: Reading a progress or risk report

Analysis result: DAMP = 66%, showing the report focuses on:

  • Causes of delay
  • Risks
  • Difficulties
  • Affecting conditions
  • Dependencies between tasks
  • Proposed solutions

The project manager should prioritize the risks, plan-altering conditions, and warnings that need early handling.

Scenario 3: Reading a project management process

Analysis result: AMP = 50%, DAMP = 49% → STABLE state.

The document standardizes a workflow, with no dominant Core Content. Users should read step by step through the process.

How to use NKTg AI in project management

  • Step 1: Observe the AMP and DAMP scores.
  • Step 2: Choose the right reading approach:
    • AMP > 55% → Read the Core Content to identify the task, owner, target, and timeline.
    • DAMP > 55% → Focus on risks, causes, conditions, and dependencies between tasks.
    • AMP ≈ DAMP (STABLE) → Read process by process or phase by phase.
  • Step 3: Build a tracking list: priority tasks, owners, timelines, risks, and topics to discuss.

Value of NKTg AI for project managers

NKTg AI doesn't replace the project manager, but helps you quickly access the most important information in the original document:

  • In meeting minutes and plans: identify tasks, owners, targets, and deadlines.
  • In progress and risk reports: identify causes of delay, affecting conditions, and warnings.
  • In management processes: recognize the STABLE state and reference step by step.

Conclusion

In project management, success depends on the ability to identify the right work and the right time to act. NKTg AI helps project managers recognize a document's structure from the start using the AMP, DAMP, and STABLE metrics, helping them choose the right approach.

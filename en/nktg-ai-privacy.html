Privacy and How NKTg AI Handles Data

NKTg AI is designed with a privacy-first principle. The entire text analysis process runs directly on the user's device via WebAssembly, with no need to send content to a server for processing.

How does NKTg AI process text?

When you type text or upload a document, NKTg AI processes the entire content right in your browser using WebAssembly technology. The text is analyzed directly on your device without being transmitted to a server.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Your text content is not sent to a server for analysis. The entire process happens locally on your device.

Does NKTg AI use cloud AI?

No. NKTg AI does not use cloud AI services to analyze text. Document content is not sent to any AI system or third-party processing service.

Is my text stored in a remote database?

No. NKTg AI does not store your text content on a server or in a remote database.

Where is history stored?

Usage history is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means history stays entirely on your device and is not synced to a server.

If you clear your browser data, reinstall your browser, or switch to a different device, the history will no longer be there.

Can I use NKTg AI without an Internet connection?

Yes. Once the NKTg AI app has loaded in your browser, you can continue analyzing text even after disconnecting from the Internet.

Note: If you reload the page or visit NKTg AI for the first time on a new device, your browser will need an Internet connection to load the app.

Why does NKTg AI use this architecture?

The local-processing architecture provides several benefits:

  • Text never leaves the user's device
  • Content is not sent to a server for analysis
  • No cloud AI is used
  • Text is not stored in a remote database
  • History is stored locally on the device
  • Analysis can continue without an Internet connection once the app has loaded

Summary

  • The entire analysis process runs directly in the browser via WebAssembly
  • Text is not sent to a server for processing
  • No cloud AI is used
  • Text is not stored in a remote database
  • History is stored in localStorage on the user's device
  • You can continue analyzing text even when your device loses its Internet connection (as long as the app was loaded before)

That last point is worth emphasizing, since it's both technically accurate and a distinct advantage of NKTg AI compared to many AI services that depend on a server to process every request.

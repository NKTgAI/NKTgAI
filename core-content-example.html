What is Core Content? A worked example

Core Content is the sentence with the highest density of executable actions and the most concrete outcome in the whole text.

It is the sentence that, if removed, causes the text to lose core information, information the reader cannot fully infer from the remaining sentences.

NKTg AI always keeps this sentence exactly as it appears in the original text — no rewording, no interpretation, no new content generated.

How is Core Content different from a summary?

This is NKTg AI's most important distinction:

Core Content Summary
An existing sentence from the original text. A sentence rewritten by AI or a human.
Keeps 100% of the original content. May add, remove, or paraphrase.
Selects only the sentence carrying the most core information. Merges ideas from multiple different sentences.
Does not change the author's meaning. May depend on the summarizer's interpretation.

In short: A summary is rewriting; Core Content is finding the single most important existing sentence.

Worked Example

Original text

The executive board decides to roll out the Central Region market expansion plan right in Q3.
The sales director is assigned a 25% revenue growth target and must finalize agreements with three strategic partners before September 30.
HR is responsible for hiring 20 additional sales staff within 45 days.
Finance must allocate a budget of 50 billion VND to support the marketing campaign.
After six months, the whole company will review results and adjust strategy to ensure sustainable growth targets.

NKTg AI's result

Core Content:
"The sales director is assigned a 25% revenue growth target and must finalize agreements with three strategic partners before September 30."

Why is this the Core Content?

Among the five sentences above:

  • Sentence 1 only states the general policy.
  • Sentence 3 is HR's supporting task.
  • Sentence 4 is Finance's supporting task.
  • Sentence 5 is the review step after rollout.

Only sentence 2 simultaneously identifies: the person responsible, the target to reach, the action to take, the concrete outcome, and the deadline. This is the sentence with the highest density of executable action and outcome.

What happens if the Core Content is removed?

Removing sentence 2, the text still indicates there is an expansion plan, hiring, budget preparation, and a review after six months. However, the reader would no longer know what the growth target is, who is primarily responsible, what work must be completed, or when the deadline is.

In other words, the text has lost its core information. That is exactly the criterion NKTg AI uses to determine Core Content.

Something to note

Core Content does not depend on its position in the text. It can appear at the beginning, in the middle, or at the end, as long as it is the sentence carrying the highest semantic energy by NKTg AI's criteria.

Conclusion

Core Content is not the opening sentence, the closing sentence, or the longest sentence. It is the original sentence containing the most core information in the text, where the density of executable action and outcome is concentrated.

NKTg AI does not summarize text — it decodes the language structure to find this sentence. That's why the result always preserves the original content, without interpretation or additions, helping the reader quickly grasp the "logical genetic code" of the whole text.
